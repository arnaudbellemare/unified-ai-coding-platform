# x402 Payment Protocol Pricing

## 🆓 **FREE x402 Benefits**

### **Coinbase x402 Facilitator**
- **✅ FREE facilitator service** - No fees for using Coinbase's x402 facilitator
- **✅ FREE gas fees** - Gas costs are sponsored by Coinbase
- **✅ FREE receiving** - Receiving payments is always free

### **CDP Server Wallets**
- **✅ 5,000 FREE operations per month** - Creating accounts, signing/sending transactions
- **💰 $0.005 per operation** after 5,000 monthly limit
- **✅ FREE receiving** - Always free to receive payments

## **Cost Breakdown**

### **For Merchants:**
- **x402 facilitator**: FREE

### **For Users:**
- **Payment processing**: FREE
- **Gas fees**: FREE (sponsored)
